Concept

APV and peer group financing approaches

Meaning. Alternative ways to assess financing mix: APV values tax benefits and distress costs directly, while peer analysis uses comparable-company behavior as evidence.

Why it matters. They check the cost-of-capital approach from different angles and reveal when market practice or model assumptions deserve skepticism.

How Damodaran develops it. The course uses APV and peer groups after WACC optimization to show that financing decisions can be triangulated rather than dictated by one model.

Common mistakes

  • Using peers as proof instead of evidence.
  • Forgetting that APV still requires judgments about debt benefits and costs.
