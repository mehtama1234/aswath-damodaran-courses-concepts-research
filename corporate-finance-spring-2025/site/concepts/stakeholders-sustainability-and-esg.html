Concept

Stakeholders, sustainability, and ESG

Meaning. The claim that firms should consider employees, customers, society, sustainability, and ESG goals alongside or inside value creation.

Why it matters. These claims matter because they can either discipline long-term thinking or become vague cover for unaccountable management choices.

How Damodaran develops it. Damodaran treats stakeholder language as a test of clarity: what exactly is being optimized, who measures it, and who pays when goals conflict?

Common mistakes

  • Using ESG as an unmeasured virtue label.
  • Pretending stakeholder tradeoffs have no cost.
