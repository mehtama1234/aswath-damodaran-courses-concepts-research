Concept

Hurdle rates

Meaning. The minimum required return for an investment, matched to its risk, currency, and financing mix.

Why it matters. Hurdle rates decide what the firm says yes or no to; they translate risk into capital allocation discipline.

How Damodaran develops it. The course treats hurdle rates as built objects: riskfree rate, risk premium, beta, debt cost, and capital mix must all be internally consistent.

Common mistakes

  • Using the same hurdle rate for every project.
  • Confusing accounting return targets with risk-adjusted required returns.
