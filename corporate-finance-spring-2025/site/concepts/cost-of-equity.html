Concept

Cost of equity

Meaning. The return equity investors require for bearing the risk of the firm's residual cash flows.

Why it matters. It is the equity hurdle rate for projects and the equity discount rate in valuation.

How Damodaran develops it. The course builds cost of equity from riskfree rates, premiums, and betas, turning risk into an explicit required return.

Common mistakes

  • Treating cost of equity as what the company wants to earn.
  • Using one corporate cost of equity for projects with different risk.
