Concept

Dividends and buybacks

Meaning. The two main ways firms return cash to shareholders, each carrying different tax, signaling, flexibility, and trust implications.

Why it matters. Payout policy is where excess cash, reinvestment discipline, and shareholder trust become visible.

How Damodaran develops it. The payout block starts with dividends and buybacks as tools, then asks when returning cash is the best use of corporate resources.

Common mistakes

  • Treating dividends as automatically good.
  • Treating buybacks as automatically manipulative or automatically value creating.
