Concept

Corporate governance

Meaning. The system that determines whether managers act for owners, themselves, or other power centers around the firm.

Why it matters. Governance is the enforcement mechanism behind the objective function; weak governance lets value-destroying choices persist.

How Damodaran develops it. The early governance sessions turn abstract value maximization into a control problem: who has power, how is it checked, and what happens when nobody is watching?

Common mistakes

  • Assuming boards automatically protect shareholders.
  • Ignoring how dispersed ownership weakens discipline.
