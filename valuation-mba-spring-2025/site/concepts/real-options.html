Concept

Real options

Meaning. A real option is the value of managerial flexibility under uncertainty when the firm owns a scarce right to wait, expand, abandon, or change course.

Why it matters. It changes the role of uncertainty: uncertainty can increase value when downside is limited and upside remains open.

How Damodaran develops it. Real options are introduced after IPOs and then applied to patents, natural resources, distressed equity, and strategic rights.

Common mistakes

  • Using option language for any uncertain growth story.
  • Ignoring the need for exclusivity and genuine flexibility.
