Concept

Bermuda Triangle of valuation

Meaning. The analyst can get lost between story, numbers, and bias unless the valuation process keeps each leg visible and testable.

Why it matters. It gives the course a practical warning: valuation errors are often failures of process rather than failures of arithmetic.

How Damodaran develops it. Early sessions use this frame to separate disciplined valuation from confirmation bias, spreadsheet theater, and market storytelling.

Common mistakes

  • Letting bias choose the assumptions.
  • Using complexity to disguise weak economics.
