Concept

Distressed equity

Meaning. Equity in a deeply distressed firm can resemble an option on the firm's assets after debt claims are considered.

Why it matters. It explains why equity can retain value even when conventional DCF equity value looks close to zero.

How Damodaran develops it. The distressed-equity session uses option logic to value residual claims when debt creates asymmetric payoffs.

Common mistakes

  • Treating distressed equity like normal equity.
  • Ignoring debt maturity, asset volatility, and claim priority.
