Concept

Relative risk and betas

Meaning. Beta is a measure of relative risk, but useful beta estimation has to reflect business mix and leverage rather than blindly trust regressions.

Why it matters. The course uses beta to discipline risk measurement while warning against false precision from noisy market data.

How Damodaran develops it. Damodaran moves from regression betas to bottom-up thinking and alternatives when firms are private, changing, or thinly traded.

Common mistakes

  • Taking a regression beta as truth.
  • Forgetting that financial leverage changes equity beta.
