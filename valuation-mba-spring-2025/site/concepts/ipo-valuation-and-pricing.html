Concept

IPO valuation and pricing

Meaning. IPOs combine intrinsic valuation, pricing, market timing, narrative momentum, underwriter incentives, and investor demand.

Why it matters. They expose the tension between what a business is worth and what the market can be persuaded to pay at issuance.

How Damodaran develops it. The IPO session closes pricing and opens real options, showing how market process can dominate clean valuation logic.

Common mistakes

  • Treating IPO price as proof of value.
  • Ignoring how supply, demand, and narrative shape offering prices.
