Concept

Stories into inputs

Meaning. Stories matter only when they become model inputs: market size, revenue growth, margins, reinvestment, risk, and terminal economics.

Why it matters. This is how the course turns strategy talk into something that can be tested, debated, and revised.

How Damodaran develops it. The narrative sessions and first valuation exercises force every qualitative claim to show up somewhere in the model.

Common mistakes

  • Letting a persuasive story float above the spreadsheet.
  • Changing inputs without changing the underlying story.
