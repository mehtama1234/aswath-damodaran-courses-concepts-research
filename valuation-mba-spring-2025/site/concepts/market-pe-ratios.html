Concept

Market PE ratios

Meaning. Market PE ratios price entire markets by relating index prices to earnings and macro expectations.

Why it matters. The concept shows that pricing logic applies at the market level too, where rates, growth, risk premiums, and mood collide.

How Damodaran develops it. The tariff and market PE session applies pricing language to market-wide multiples and macro narratives.

Common mistakes

  • Reading market PE without considering rates and growth.
  • Treating macro narratives as if they bypass valuation math.
