Concept

Peer group selection

Meaning. A peer group is an analytical choice about which firms are similar enough for relative pricing.

Why it matters. Peer choice is where pricing becomes both useful and dangerous: the answer can move with the comparable set.

How Damodaran develops it. Pricing analytics sessions use peer groups and regressions to make relative pricing more disciplined.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming peers are naturally given.
  • Picking peers because they support the target price.
