Concept

Equity risk premiums

Meaning. The equity risk premium is the market price of bearing equity risk over a riskfree asset.

Why it matters. It is one of the biggest levers in valuation and should reflect current market pricing, not just inherited historical averages.

How Damodaran develops it. Damodaran develops historical, implied, and country-adjusted premiums as competing ways to estimate the market price of risk.

Common mistakes

  • Using a stale historical premium without checking current market levels.
  • Ignoring country risk in global businesses.
