Concept

Liquidity and ownership

Meaning. Liquidity, diversification, and control rights affect value when the owner cannot freely trade or diversify the asset.

Why it matters. This shifts valuation from a clean security-market problem to a problem of who owns the asset and under what constraints.

How Damodaran develops it. Private-company and control sessions make ownership frictions explicit rather than treating them as footnotes.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the same discount for every private firm.
  • Ignoring whether the buyer is diversified or has control.
