Concept

Cash-flow consistency

Meaning. Cash flows have to match the claim being valued, the currency chosen, the inflation assumption, and the discount rate.

Why it matters. Most wrong valuations are not wrong because the math fails; they are wrong because the model combines incompatible assumptions.

How Damodaran develops it. The early DCF sessions make consistency the hidden law that governs currency, nominal versus real inputs, taxes, debt, and equity claims.

Common mistakes

  • Discounting equity cash flows at a cost of capital.
  • Mixing real cash flows with nominal discount rates.
