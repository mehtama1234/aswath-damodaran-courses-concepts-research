Valuation Is Taught As A Sequenced Craft, Not A Mystery · sessions 1, 2, 3
The course opens by organizing the valuation universe before choosing methods

Early sessions introduce valuation approaches, class structure, and the boundaries of what each method can and cannot do before the course commits to DCF as its main language.

Valuation Is Taught As A Sequenced Craft, Not A Mystery · sessions 2, 3
The Bermuda Triangle is confusion between value, price, and narrative

One of the first conceptual jobs of the course is to stop students from treating every market number as value and every valuation choice as interchangeable.

Valuation Is Taught As A Sequenced Craft, Not A Mystery · sessions 4
Valuation is presented as learnable through ordered inputs

The DCF big-picture session treats valuation as a sequence of buildable inputs rather than as a mysterious spreadsheet inherited from experts.

Discount Rates Are Built From Economic Logic · sessions 4, 5
Riskfree rates depend on currency and inflation regime

The course insists that the riskfree rate is not copied blindly from US Treasuries, but chosen to match the currency and nominal setting of the valuation.

Discount Rates Are Built From Economic Logic · sessions 5, 6
Equity risk premiums are estimated, not memorized

Students are pushed beyond historical averages into implied equity risk premiums and country-sensitive judgment about what the market is demanding for risk.

Discount Rates Are Built From Economic Logic · sessions 7, 8
Betas and debt costs connect company risk back to market pricing

Relative risk, betas, and the first steps on cost of debt make discount rates feel like operating claims about the business, not detached finance formulas.

Cash Flows, Growth, And Terminal Value Form The Operating Core · sessions 8, 9, 10
Cash-flow definitions require accounting cleanup

Tax rates, capex, working capital, and FCFE closure are used to show that valuation begins by translating accounting statements into economic cash-flow claims.

Cash Flows, Growth, And Terminal Value Form The Operating Core · sessions 10, 11
Growth only matters when the reinvestment burden is understood

The growth sessions repeatedly connect expansion to reinvestment requirements rather than letting students treat growth as a free input.

Cash Flows, Growth, And Terminal Value Form The Operating Core · sessions 11, 12, 13
Terminal value is where hidden sloppiness becomes visible

The standalone terminal-value material makes clear that sloppy steady-state assumptions can dominate the valuation even after long explicit forecasts.

Storytelling Is Allowed, But It Must Change The Numbers · sessions 13, 14
Storytelling enters only after valuation loose ends are handled

The course delays narrative until students have enough mechanical footing to understand what a story is supposed to control in a valuation.

Storytelling Is Allowed, But It Must Change The Numbers · sessions 14, 15, 16
A business story must alter company-specific assumptions

The first storytelling and company-valuation sessions make narrative concrete by forcing it into growth, margins, reinvestment, and life-cycle assumptions.

Storytelling Is Allowed, But It Must Change The Numbers · sessions 16, 17, 18
Special company types stress-test whether the story is robust

Declining firms, emerging-market firms, and financial-service companies are used to show that storytelling is constrained by business type and context.

Pricing Is A Different Language From Intrinsic Value · sessions 18, 19
Intrinsic value and pricing are separated rather than blurred together

The transition into pricing is explicit: the course treats it as a different language with different strengths and vulnerabilities from DCF valuation.

Pricing Is A Different Language From Intrinsic Value · sessions 19, 21
Pricing analytics depend on peer groups and hidden assumptions

The peer-group material emphasizes that multiples do not remove assumptions. They move those assumptions into relative judgments about comparability.

Pricing Is A Different Language From Intrinsic Value · sessions 21, 22
Asset-based valuation and pricing closure reveal where markets anchor

The pricing closure sessions show how markets often rely on simpler anchors when intrinsic value is hard to observe or defend directly.

Special Cases Show Where Default Models Stop Working · sessions 23
Private-company valuation changes when owners are not marginal market investors

Private-company valuation forces students to confront illiquidity, control, and undiversified ownership rather than pretending every asset lives in a public market.

Special Cases Show Where Default Models Stop Working · sessions 24
IPO cases expose the pull of market mood and issuance context

The IPO closure sessions highlight how institutional demand and market climate can distort the relationship between intrinsic value and observed pricing.

Special Cases Show Where Default Models Stop Working · sessions 27
Acquisition and value-enhancement cases move valuation toward governance and strategic change

The course closes by showing that acquisition value and value enhancement depend on decision changes, not just on passively measuring current operations.

Uncertainty Can Create Optionality, Not Just A Higher Discount Rate · sessions 24
Real options begin where immediate project viability ends

The introductory options material uses IPO closure and real-options transition points to show that a bad project today can still contain value if the right to wait is scarce.

Uncertainty Can Create Optionality, Not Just A Higher Discount Rate · sessions 25
Patents and resource rights make contingency visible

Patents and natural resources are used because they make exclusivity, timing choice, and contingent upside easier to see than in ordinary operating assets.

Uncertainty Can Create Optionality, Not Just A Higher Discount Rate · sessions 26
Distressed equity is valuable because claims can behave like options

The distressed-equity session widens the options lens beyond technology cases and shows how financial distress can turn equity into a contingent claim on recovery.

The Undergraduate Course Treats Valuation As A Doable Project · sessions 1, 2
Students are pushed to pick companies early and stop deferring commitment

Damodaran repeatedly uses class logistics to force movement: choose a company, start the valuation, and stop waiting for perfect certainty before acting.

The Undergraduate Course Treats Valuation As A Doable Project · sessions 14, 23
The course keeps tying lecture content back to the live semester project

Project submissions, feedback windows, quizzes, and master spreadsheets are woven into the class as a way of making valuation execution concrete.

The Undergraduate Course Treats Valuation As A Doable Project · sessions 23, 27
Valuation ends with a recommendation, not only with a number

By the end of the course students are expected to connect DCF value, pricing, and recommendation into a final judgment rather than stopping at mechanical output.
