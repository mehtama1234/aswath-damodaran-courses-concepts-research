Real options

Real options treat uncertainty as a potential source of value when a firm controls a scarce right to wait, expand, or change course.

Primary sessions: 25, 14, 26, 24, 3 patents-and-resource-optionsdistressed-equity-as-optionintrinsic-value-versus-pricing

Core Idea

Real options treat uncertainty as a potential source of value when a firm controls a scarce right to wait, expand, or change course.

Meaning

Real options treat uncertainty as a potential source of value when a firm controls a scarce right to wait, expand, or change course.

Development

The IPO-to-options transition and the patents/resources block show when optionality is real and when it is being abused rhetorically.

Why It Matters

It is the course's clearest break from the idea that uncertainty only belongs in a higher discount rate.
