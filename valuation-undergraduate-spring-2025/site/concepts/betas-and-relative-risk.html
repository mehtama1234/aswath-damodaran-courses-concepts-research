Meaning

Beta is used as a way to connect company risk back to market risk, but only after thinking through business mix and leverage.

Development

The beta sessions push students toward relative-risk reasoning before debt costs and cost of capital are finalized.

Why It Matters

It prevents discount rates from becoming abstract finance formulas detached from the business being valued.
