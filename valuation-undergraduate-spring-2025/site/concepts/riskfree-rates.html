Riskfree rates

Core Idea

The riskfree rate is chosen to match the currency and inflation setting of the valuation, not copied blindly from a default reference bond.

Meaning

The riskfree rate is chosen to match the currency and inflation setting of the valuation, not copied blindly from a default reference bond.

Development

The early rate sessions repeatedly tie the riskfree rate back to currency, sovereign default risk, and inflation logic.

Why It Matters

It anchors every later discount-rate choice, so a bad starting rate contaminates the whole model.
