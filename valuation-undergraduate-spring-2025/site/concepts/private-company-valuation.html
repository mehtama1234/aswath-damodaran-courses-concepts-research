Concept Page

Private-company valuation

Private-company valuation changes when the owners are undiversified, liquidity is limited, and control rights are structured differently from public markets.

Primary sessions: 23, 24, 22, 26, 21 asset-based-valuationacquisition-and-value-enhancementbetas-and-relative-risk

Core Idea

Private-company valuation changes when the owners are undiversified, liquidity is limited, and control rights are structured differently from public markets.

Meaning

Private-company valuation changes when the owners are undiversified, liquidity is limited, and control rights are structured differently from public markets.

Development

The private-company session turns owner type, control, and illiquidity into central valuation variables.

Why It Matters

It forces the class to confront who owns the asset and under what constraints rather than assuming a public-market marginal investor.
