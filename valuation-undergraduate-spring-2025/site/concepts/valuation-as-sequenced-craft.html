Core Idea

Meaning

Valuation is taught as a learnable sequence of choices rather than a mysterious expert ritual.

Development

The early sessions keep breaking valuation into ordered steps, then the later sessions return to that sequence when the class reaches pricing, options, and acquisitions.

Why It Matters

This is the pedagogical spine of the undergraduate course and the reason the material feels executable rather than ornamental.
