Convolution on grids

The grids lecture recasts familiar image convolution as one especially regular case of geometric deep learning.

gridscnntranslation

Core Idea

Grid domains support strong forms of translation symmetry and weight sharing, which makes convolution natural rather than arbitrary.

Why It Matters

It prevents students from treating CNNs as the default model for everything. They become one domain-matched answer among many.

Why It Works

The grid gives a stable notion of nearby pixels and translation-consistent filters.

What It Teaches

Classical deep learning already contains geometry when the domain makes it obvious.

Linked Subthemes
  • Grids recover classical convolution as one structured special case
  • Groups and homogeneous spaces make global symmetry computational
Broader Patterns
  • The regularity of images hides how special the grid case really is.
  • The broader field generalizes convolution by loosening what counts as a neighborhood or symmetry.
Applied Uses
  • Image and signal tasks on regular lattices.
  • Explaining how CNN intuitions map into graph or group settings.
