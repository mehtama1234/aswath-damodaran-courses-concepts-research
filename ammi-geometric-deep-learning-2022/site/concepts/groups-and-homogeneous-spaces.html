Groups and homogeneous spaces

Groups and homogeneous spaces formalize global transformation structure so equivariant operators can be defined with precision.

groupshomogeneous-spacesequivariant-operators

Core Idea

A domain may be acted on by a symmetry group, and that action can be used to define how features and filters should transform.

Why It Matters

This is where the course moves from intuition about symmetry to a deeper operator-design toolkit.

Algebraic Layer

The group tells you how transformations compose.

Geometric Layer

The homogeneous space tells you where those transformations act.

Applied Uses
