Concept Page

Gauge equivariance

Gauge equivariance handles problems where local frames matter but no single global frame is valid.

gaugeslocal-framesequivariance

Core Idea

Features can be represented relative to local coordinate frames as long as the model transforms them consistently under changes of gauge.

Why It Matters

It is one of the most conceptually advanced points in the course and shows why local consistency can replace global symmetry.

Problem

There is no globally preferred frame.

Solution

Use local frames and enforce transformation consistency across them.

Linked Subthemes
  • Gauge equivariance replaces one global frame with compatible local ones
  • Later talks push symmetry beyond textbook global actions
Broader Patterns
  • Local geometric structure can be more important than any one global coordinate system.
  • Advanced equivariant design is often about compatibility between overlapping local descriptions.
Applied Uses
  • Directional features on surfaces or manifolds.
  • Domains where tangent-space information matters.
