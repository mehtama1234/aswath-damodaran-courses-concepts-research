Argument Spine

1. High-dimensional learning needs inductive bias. 2. Geometric priors encode that bias by respecting transformations and relations in the domain. 3. Symmetry, invariance, and equivariance are the central control language for structured learning. 4. Different domains require different operators: sets, graphs, and grids are not interchangeable. 5. Manifolds, gauges, and beyond-group ideas generalize the field beyond flat global coordinates. 6. The frontier is judged by working applications in science and complex structured prediction, not by abstraction alone.
