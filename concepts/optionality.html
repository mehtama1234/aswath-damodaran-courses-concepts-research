Normalized Concept

Optionality

Damodaran repeatedly returns to the value of future flexibility, but he treats optionality differently depending on whether the decision maker is an investor looking at growth or a manager choosing projects.

Foundations of Finance

Options as contingent claims

Optionality enters at the introductory level as a way of showing that some assets are valuable because they pay off only in particular states of the world, which changes valuation logic before any advanced modeling begins.
