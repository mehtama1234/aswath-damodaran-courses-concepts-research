Normalized Concept

Risk

Risk is one of the clearest recurring Damodaran concepts across courses. It appears as a first-principles condition for return, a lived investor tradeoff, a formal corporate finance input, and a valuation building block, with the undergraduate valuation course adding a more visibly scaffolded route into discount-rate logic.

Cross-course pattern: Across the courses Damodaran uses risk to do linked jobs: explain the basic bargain behind return, show how investors live with uncertainty, show how firms price uncertainty into decisions, teach students how discount-rate machinery is assembled step by step, and build the more compressed professional valuation architecture that rests on that machinery.
