Valuation Undergraduate Spring 2025

Valuation as a sequenced and doable craft

Valuation is taught as a buildable sequence that students can execute step by step: frame the problem, separate value from price, build rates from economic logic, translate accounting into cash flows, connect growth to reinvestment, and finish with pricing, optionality, acquisitions, and recommendation-making.

Foundations of Finance

Valuation begins with claim design

Valuation is introduced by asking what kind of claim is being priced. Bonds, equity, and options require different logic because contractual, residual, and contingent claims carry different payoff structures.

Cross-course pattern: In foundations valuation starts by separating the logic of different claims; in the investor course it arbitrates between competing philosophies; in the corporate finance course it integrates managerial decisions; in the undergraduate valuation course it becomes a highly scaffolded craft that students can execute in sequence; and in the MBA valuation course it appears in its more compressed professional form as a continuous operating language connecting narrative, numbers, pricing, optionality, and judgment.
