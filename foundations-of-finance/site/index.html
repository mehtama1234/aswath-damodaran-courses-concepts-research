A large share of the visible course material is about showing why finance privileges cash flows over accounting earnings and why time changes the value of every claim.

Risk Is Inescapable, But It Must Be Measured Through The Right Eyes

The risk block argues that risk is not only danger to be avoided; it is the unavoidable condition for earning return, and the real challenge is to measure and price it properly.

Valuation Starts By Understanding What Kind Of Claim You Own

The course builds valuation from the ground up by distinguishing contractual, residual, and contingent claims and then showing how each maps into a different pricing structure.

Macro Forces, Inflation, Rates, And Currencies Shape Every Financial Decision

The late visible sessions make the case that inflation, interest rates, and currencies are not background variables. They are the environment that determines what discount rates mean and how cross-border comparisons work.
