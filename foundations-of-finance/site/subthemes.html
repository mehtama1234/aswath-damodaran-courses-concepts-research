Finance Is A Forward-Looking Language Of Business · sessions 1
Finance cuts across every business function

Strategy, marketing, operations, and communication still come back to money, which is why finance sits at the center rather than at the edge of business thinking.

Finance Is A Forward-Looking Language Of Business · sessions 2
The financial balance sheet cares about future value, not accounting buckets

Assets are grouped into assets in place and growth assets, and financing is simplified into debt and equity so the analyst can focus on how value is created rather than how line items are labeled.

Finance Is A Forward-Looking Language Of Business · sessions 2
Growth only matters when it creates value

The course introduces growth assets as valuable only when future investments earn more than their cost of capital, making value creation the real test of expansion.

Cash Flows And Time Translate Economic Choices Into Value · sessions 1
Cash flow is the economic core beneath accounting form

The course repeatedly distinguishes finance from accounting by insisting that value comes from the cash generated by assets, not from reported earnings alone.

Cash Flows And Time Translate Economic Choices Into Value · sessions 6
Time value is the bridge from intuition to pricing

A dollar today is worth more than a dollar later because delay has an economic cost, and finance operationalizes that intuition through present-value mechanics.

Cash Flows And Time Translate Economic Choices Into Value · sessions 7, 8
Different cash-flow claims produce different valuation logics

Contractual claims such as bonds, residual claims such as equity, and contingent claims such as options are valued differently because the timing and certainty of their cash flows differ.

Risk Is Inescapable, But It Must Be Measured Through The Right Eyes · sessions 4
Risk has a positive and negative side

Great businesses are not built by avoiding all risk but by taking the right risks, which is why the course defines risk as dual rather than purely harmful.

Risk Is Inescapable, But It Must Be Measured Through The Right Eyes · sessions 4, 5
Risk aversion is what makes finance matter

The logic of risk premia starts from the observation that people demand compensation for uncertainty and that the required compensation varies across investors.

Risk Is Inescapable, But It Must Be Measured Through The Right Eyes · sessions 5, 8
Required return depends on the claim and the holder

As the course moves into equity valuation, the required return is tied to the risk seen by equity investors in a residual claim rather than treated as an arbitrary discount number.

Valuation Starts By Understanding What Kind Of Claim You Own · sessions 7
Bonds are contractual claims discounted at rates that reflect default and timing

Bond valuation is used to teach how known coupons and face value can be translated into price through discounting, yields, and interest-rate sensitivity.

Valuation Starts By Understanding What Kind Of Claim You Own · sessions 8
Equity is a residual claim on what is left after everyone else is paid

Equity valuation begins by recognizing that stockholders do not own promised cash flows; they own what survives after debt payments, reinvestment, and every other prior claim.

Valuation Starts By Understanding What Kind Of Claim You Own · sessions 9
Options reveal how contingency changes value logic

The option-valuation introduction widens the course beyond fixed and residual claims, showing that some assets are valuable because they pay off only when particular states of the world occur.

Macro Forces, Inflation, Rates, And Currencies Shape Every Financial Decision · sessions 10
Inflation is a hidden tax on financial claims

Nominal returns can look healthy while real purchasing power erodes, which is why the course insists on separating inflation from real economic gain.

Macro Forces, Inflation, Rates, And Currencies Shape Every Financial Decision · sessions 11
Interest rates are built from real growth and expected inflation

The Fisher equation is used to break visible borrowing and lending rates into the deeper forces that move them across time and across instruments.

Macro Forces, Inflation, Rates, And Currencies Shape Every Financial Decision · sessions 13
Currencies differ mainly because inflation differs

Currency analysis is anchored in inflation, interest-rate parity, and purchasing-power logic, leading to the broader lesson that analysis should be currency-consistent rather than attached to one nominal unit.
