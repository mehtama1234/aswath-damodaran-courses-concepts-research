The Deep Track

8 first-principles deep dives that teach valuation by doing it. Each starts from a plain question, builds the idea in everyday words, and shows it on a real discounted-cash-flow engine we actually ran on one worked company — with computed numbers, an honest weakness, and where the idea reappears. The inputs are assumptions (valuation always is); the arithmetic is real, and the same engine values any company by plugging in its figures.
