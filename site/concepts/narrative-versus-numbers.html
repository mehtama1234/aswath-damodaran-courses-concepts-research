Normalized Concept

Narrative Versus Numbers

A recurring Damodaran tension is that stories matter, but they only become useful when disciplined by numbers, incentives, explicit assumptions, and the actual structure of the claim being discussed. The undergraduate valuation course adds a particularly visible teaching bridge between narrative and model inputs.
