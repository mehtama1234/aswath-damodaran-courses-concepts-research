Recurring Damodaran Ideas Across Courses

1. Financial Grammar Before Specialization

Across the active course set, Foundations of Finance does the earliest explanatory work. It introduces the grammar that later courses assume: business structure, cash flows, time, claims, and nominal consistency. The later courses each specialize that grammar into a different decision domain, with the two valuation courses showing how the same architecture is taught at two audience levels.

Finance as a forward-looking business language

Finance is introduced as a way of reading businesses through assets, growth, claims, cash flows, and value through time.

Investment Philosophies makes risk lived and behavioral. Corporate Finance makes it operational. The two valuation courses make it architectural, with different levels of scaffolding.
